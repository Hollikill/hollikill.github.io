Edward was sitting in the lobby of the inn, snacking on some fruit.

It had been relatively easy to find, the building indeed wider than most surrounding it to accommodate all the rooms for possible tenants. Large windows covered the exterior walls, allowing large amount of skylight into the rooms and lobby. After signing the usage book[^1], Edward had moved his bags upstairs to an open room, then come back down to the lobby to have lunch.

The lobby had shelves all along each wall, sparsely decorated with knick-knack of various kinds, seemingly from across the Empire. Little metal sculptures, some books, a cylinder of Lux within very clear glass, and more. Windows all along the whole facade lent a open feel to the building, the street visible through frosted glass.

Edward had a map open in front of him. He had picked it up at the town hall, and it seemed to cover mostly the major landmarks of the town, rather than specific businesses. The manufactory was listed, the event plaza, the town hall itself, the street layout[^2], and an arrow pointing north off the map to the Mt. Verdine lookout point.

Though he needed natural trees as the foundations of his house, the manufactory was sure to have the standard flex work facility where he could find someone to help with construction.
