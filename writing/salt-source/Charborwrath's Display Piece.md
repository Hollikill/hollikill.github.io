---
Demon Level: 3
---
Spell that 'freezes' something in a single state, including living beings.

The power of eldritch transformation will bend the affected object or area back into a close facsimile of the original state. Sufficient time or effort to resist the stasis of the spell will end the spell.

Does not actually put affected things into time-stasis, just the continuous overwriting of reality makes it seem sort of like it. Prolonged duration under this spell, especially with a more mobile thing state, can irrevocably transform the affected object or area into a different form.

Examples:
1. A flowing river affected by this spell will be able to keep flowing. If you stopped the water, the spell would actually intervene to restart it, because the 'flowing river' is considered one object.
2. A person attempting to resist the spell may come out looking like a deformed version of a completely different person. Note: in the example, the person can choose to resist or not because the mind stays within the same idea of 'this person's brain'.
