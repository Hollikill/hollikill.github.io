Rules

 

Setup

Players each take one of the four tokens and place them at the start space, marked "Start". Players choose among themselves to assign each of themselves one of the 4 character roles (listed below).

 

Each player rolls a six-sided die. They player with the least number takes their turn first. If there is a tie, tied players roll again.

 

Turns

Players roll a six-sided dice and advance that many spaces on the board. They may land on a space with a symbol, in which case they perform the effect correlated with that symbol (listed below).

 

After a player is done with their turn, the player to their left goes next.

 

Winning

Players win the game by reaching the final space, marked "End". Second, Third, and Fourth, place positions are determined by who is closest to the final space when the game is won.

 

Special Spaces

Each space is one of 7 colors, and may have a letter or symbol on it to represent a special effect players must follow.

 

Charade space

Represented by letter "C"

This space only has an effect if the player moves onto from the initial roll on their turn.

 

1. the current player must do charades for 40 seconds. If their teammate can figure it out, both the current player and guesser advance 6 spaces without doing the action on the space.

2. Player randomly draws charades from the charade pile

3. The current player cannot speak during charades (unless they are the oracle)

 

Charade Teams

Players sitting opposite each other are on a charades team together. If people change spots, the teams change with the spots.

 

Arrow space

Represented by letter "L"

 

1. the player slides down the arrow to an earlier space

2. Arrow is visually drawn on the board

 

Stop space

Represented by letter "S"

 

1. the player must skip their next turn

 

Lance Space

Represented by spiral icon

 

1. The player becomes the Cylancer. The previous Cylancer takes the new Cylancer's old character role.

2. the previous Cylancer must swap positions with the player.

 

Event space

Represented by letter "E"

This space only has an effect if the player moves onto from the initial roll on their turn.

 

1. The player opposite the current player secretly draws an event from the event pile (they are called the reading player). It will have one of 3 actions the other players (they are called the event players) must try to do:

1. Five: players must high five another event player. The event player who did not high five must go back 3 spaces.

2. Up Top: event players look up and cross their hands in an X shape on their chest. The event player who stands first advances 3 spaces. In case of a tie, each winning player advances 1 space.

3. Sherry: event players must mime fancily drinking a glass of water. They must have their pinky finger extended outward. The event player who does this first advances 3 spaces. In case of a tie, each winning player advances 1 space.

2. If the reading player accidentally reveals the event or reads it wrong, they go back 3 spaces. If they read it right, they advance 1 space.

 

Rotate space

Represented by letter "R"

 

1. the current player must stand up and exchange seats with the player to the left of them.

 
 

Character Roles

Each player has 1 of 4 character roles. Each character role has special rules to follow, and can do 1 ability to advance in the game

 

The Pedant

1. the pedant has two six-sided dice. They can roll them as frequently as possible, but must wait for them to settle to reroll. If they roll two ones, they may perform their ability. They only start rolling again once they have tried to use their ability.

2. The pedant's ability is calling out a rule violation one of the other players has made before the game moves on to the next turn (or 30 seconds, if it happens at the end of the turn). If the pedant is right, they advance two spaces. If they are wrong, they go back 1 space.

 

The Oracle

1. the oracle may speak in simple geometric shapes and colors during the charades, and must stay completely still (other than their mouth) during charades. If the oracle's charades is guessed, they advance an extra 3 spaces.

1. Example simple geometric shapes:

1. line, dot, circle, square, cross, pentagon, etc.

2. sphere, cube, pyramid, rectangular prism, cylinder, dome, dodecahedron, torus, cone, tetrahedron, icosahedron, octahedron, etc.

2. At all times, the oracle keeps track of the last word they said. If anyone says that word, the oracle may advance 2 spaces, and the player who said the word must go back 2 spaces

1. this does not apply if the oracle speaks just as another player says a word

 

The Blind

1. The blind cannot speak at all during gameplay. However, they advance twice as many spaces as everyone else at all times.

2. The blind player may speak if:

1. they are guessing for charades

2. they are reading out the event for an event space

 

The Cylancer

1. The Cylancer is specially in control of the Cylance token.

2. Once per turn, at the beginning or end of their turn, the Cylancer may place the Cylance on one of the board spaces. If another player lands on a space of this color or right next to the space with the Cylance, the Cylancer moves forward 4 spaces then swaps character roles with them. If the Cylancer lands on a space of this color or right next to the space with the Cylance, they may immediately move the Cylance again.
