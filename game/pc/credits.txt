Progress Cultivator
Created in whole by Hollister Ream

This game was largely inspired by the amazing idle game Progress Knight Quest (https://indomit.github.io/progress_knight_2/) by indomit, though no code was used from the game.
- Progress Knight Quest is based on Progress Knight 2.0 (https://symb1.github.io/progress_knight_2/) by Symb1
- Progress Knight 2.0 is based on Progress Knight (https://ihtasham42.github.io/progress-knight/) by ihtasham42

Additional inspiration for lore and features were drawn from these web serials:
- The Dao of Reincarnation: Ascending Through a Hundred Lives (https://www.royalroad.com/fiction/154239/the-dao-of-reincarnation-ascending-through-a-hundred) by RighteousEgg
- Blood Inheritance (https://www.royalroad.com/fiction/129093/blood-inheritance-a-botched-reincarnation-xianxia) by Daoist_Kavtech

No AI was used in the creation of this project
